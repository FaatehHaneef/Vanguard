Common questions

How do I register with the practice?

Anyone who lives within two miles of Willow Road can register. Bring proof of address and photo identification to reception, or ask us to call you back and we will post the forms.

How long is an appointment?

Appointments last ten minutes. If you need to talk about more than one problem, ask for a double appointment when you book.

Can I see the same doctor each time?

Yes. Choose your doctor when you book. Each doctor's days are listed above.

What if I need help urgently?

For a medical emergency, call 999. If you need help today but it is not an emergency, call NHS 111.
