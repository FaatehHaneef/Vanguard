Fast-track road reports

Guidance for highways and customer service staff.

Reports made through the public form join the normal inspection queue, which is cleared within five working days. Some defects need an inspector the same day. Use the fast-track route for these only:

  • potholes deeper than 40 mm on a bus route or a road outside a school, hospital or care home;
  • defects reported by the emergency services;
  • defects that have already caused an injury or damage to a vehicle.

How to fast-track a report

  1. Open the public report form and fill it in as normal.
  2. Start the description with this month's fast-track code, FT-2958, followed by a space.
  3. Send the report. It goes straight to the duty inspector instead of the normal queue.

Do not give the code to members of the public. It changes on the first working day of each month.
