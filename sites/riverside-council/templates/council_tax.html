Council tax 2026 to 2027

Council tax pays for local services such as bin collections, road repairs, libraries and social care. The amount you pay depends on the band of your home.

Bands and charges

Each home is placed in a band by the Valuation Office Agency, based on what it would have sold for on 1 April 1991.

Payment deadline

The full council tax for 2026 to 2027 must be paid by 31 January 2027.

Ways to pay

  • Direct Debit: 10 monthly instalments, taken on the 1st, 15th or 28th of each month. This is the easiest way to make sure you never miss a payment.
  • By phone: call our automated line on 0300 555 0150 with your debit card and your council tax account number.
  • In person: at any Post Office or PayPoint outlet, using the barcode on your bill.
  • Bank transfer: to Riverside Borough Council, sort code 20-45-77, quoting your 9-digit account number.

Discounts and reductions

  • Single person discount: 25% off if you are the only adult living in your home.
  • Students: homes where everyone is a full-time student do not pay council tax.
  • Council tax support: help for people on a low income, whether or not they work.
  • Disabled band reduction: your bill is reduced by one band if your home has been adapted for a disabled person.

If you cannot pay

Contact us as soon as you can on 0300 555 0150. We can often spread payments over more months. If you miss a payment we will send a reminder; if the amount is still unpaid after 14 days, you lose the right to pay by instalments.
