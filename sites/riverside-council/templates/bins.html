Common questions

Can I get an extra recycling bin?

Yes. Households of five or more people can ask for a second blue bin at no charge. Call 0300 555 0140 and we will deliver it within ten working days.

How do I subscribe to garden waste collections?

The garden waste service costs £48 a year and runs from March to November, with brown bins collected every other week. Subscriptions for 2027 open on 1 February 2027.

What do I do with large items such as a sofa?

We collect up to three large items for £35. Book by phone, and leave the items outside your home by 7:00 on the day we give you.

My bin is damaged. Will you replace it?

If your bin was damaged by our crew, we replace it free of charge. Otherwise a new black or blue bin costs £25, delivered.
